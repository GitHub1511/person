| policies used | 12 of 55 |
| simulation speed | ~0.37× real time, ~3 ms per physics step |

The free energy of the forward model decreases from approximately 35 to approximately 0.06 within the first
second, which corresponds to the formation of the body schema. The agent is initialised with the prior
that the sensory state does not change unless the agent itself acts and, within about ten cognitive ticks, has
learned the sensory consequences of its own commands.
