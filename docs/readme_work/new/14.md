## 14. Statement of limitations

* **Vision is implemented as a 48×36 retina with hand-designed features**, not a
  learned encoder. It preserves foveal/peripheral structure, saccades, blinks
  and the pupillary reflex, but it is not a model of early visual cortex.
* **Audition is synthesised** from contact transients and self-motion, because
  MuJoCo provides no acoustic field. No real sound propagation is modelled.
* **The forward model is linear (recursive least squares, RLS).** It captures
  the local input–output structure of the body well, but it cannot represent
  contact discontinuities or multi-step dynamics. A sequence model would be the
  natural extension.
* **Contact is modelled as rigid-body contact.** Skin compliance is represented
  in the *receptor* layer (indentation, contact area, slip) rather than by soft
  bodies, so the computed contact forces are stiffer than those of biological
  tissue.
* **The policy library is hand-authored**, not discovered. Active inference
  selects among 55 programs; it does not synthesise new ones.
* **Free-energy magnitudes are not calibrated** to any physical unit; only
  relative differences determine behaviour.
* **Self-touch is spatially coarse.** Contacts are attributed to taxels
  (tactile sensing elements) by a Gaussian receptive field, so a hand resting on
  the chest produces a spatially smooth activation rather than a resolved,
  fingerprint-like pattern.
* **Walking is achieved, but not reliably** (see §16.2 for the measured
  values): the new whole-body-control gait completes only a small number of
  steps and falls in a large fraction of longer or stop-and-go walks. The
  earlier central pattern generator (CPG) remains gated off.
* All components described in §16 (skills, hands, the mind) are *more recent and
  less extensively measured* than the sensory and affective machinery described
  above; their limitations are listed in §16.6.
* The components described in §17 (complexity, eyes, inner world, behaviour
  space) were added later still; their limitations are given in §17.7. §18
  consists of code that modifies this repository autonomously and has not been
  reviewed by a person.
